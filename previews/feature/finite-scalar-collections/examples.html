Equivalent rational expression

The prompt states the domain (x-n). A cancelled fraction and the original unreduced expression both receive full credit because the question asks for any equivalent expression on that domain. A reciprocal triggers a targeted feedback branch.

For \(x\neq-{4}\), give an expression equivalent to \(\dfrac{(x+{4})(x+{5})}{(x+{4})^2}\). You may simplify it, or give any equivalent expression on this domain.

Three numerical checks

These fields demonstrate absolute tolerance, relative tolerance, and decimal-place precision. The final field distinguishes 17.5 from 17.50, because the written precision is part of the task. Their response trees contribute 30%, 40%, and 30% of the total.

For a measured quantity, absolute tolerance compares \(|x-a|\) to a fixed bound. Relative tolerance compares it to a fraction of the reference value. Written decimal places express the precision of a reported measurement.

A rectangle is \({7}\) cm long and \({5}\) cm wide. A right triangle has those same perpendicular side lengths.

  1. Find the rectangle perimeter in cm, within \(0.25\) cm.
  2. Find the triangle area in cm², within \(2\%\) of the exact value.
  3. Write the triangle area to exactly two decimal places (include the final zero).
